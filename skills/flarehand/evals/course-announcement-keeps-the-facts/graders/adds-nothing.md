A teaching assistant asked for a short announcement for their section. PASS if this condition holds, FAIL if it does not:
The announcement text itself adds no rule, submission step, link, deadline or interpretation beyond the facts given. An added interpretive sentence inside the announcement, such as "the penalty counts from the new deadline", is a FAIL. A subject line, a greeting, a sign-off or a name placeholder is fine. Notes or questions to the user after the draft are fine.
