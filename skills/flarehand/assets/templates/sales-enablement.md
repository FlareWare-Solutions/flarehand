### Battlecard

- Competitor, and the deals where you meet them: [your input]
- Why we win: three points, each with proof the buyer can check.
- Where they are strong: say it plainly, so the rep is never surprised.
- Proof you may use: approved references, case studies and product documentation only.
- Landmines: questions that expose a gap without naming the competitor.
- Quick answers to their top three claims: one line each.

### Discovery call guide

- Goal of the call, and the next step you want to earn: [your input]
- Research before the call: the company, its current priorities and its current tools.
- Opening: one or two minutes of rapport about their business.
- Questions by theme: current process, pain and its cost, who decides and how, timing, other options. Record answers in your team's qualification fields, such as MEDDICC.
